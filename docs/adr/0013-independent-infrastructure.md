# ADR 0013 — No dependency on ClickDialog infrastructure
**Status:** proposed · **Date:** 2026-11-04

## Decision (proposed)
No dependency on ClickDialog infrastructure.

## Why
Standalone repo, database, worker, credentials and deployment avoid contaminating production systems.

## Tradeoff
No immediate reuse of existing billing/customer infrastructure.

## Acceptance / reconsideration
Managed edition integration is deferred until after MVP acceptance.

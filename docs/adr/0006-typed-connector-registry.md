# ADR 0006 — Typed connector manifest and execution adapter
**Status:** proposed · **Date:** 2026-11-04

## Decision (proposed)
Typed connector manifest and execution adapter.

## Why
Clear input/output schemas and permission declaration make connectors auditable and testable.

## Tradeoff
Registry needs versioning and backwards compatibility.

## Acceptance / reconsideration
Review after first two connectors are built.
